Delete your AgriConnect account

This page explains how to request deletion of your AgriConnect account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the AgriConnect Android app and the AgriConnect API service.

1. Delete your account from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the AgriConnect app and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the request. You may be asked to re-enter your password.

Your account is then scheduled for deletion and enters a 14-day grace period. During this window you can sign in and cancel the request if you change your mind.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@agriconnect.bw from the email address on your account, or send an SMS to [SUPPORT NUMBER], including:

  • the phone number registered to your account, and
  • your full name as it appears in the app.

We may ask you to verify control of the account (for example by a one-time code) before we action the request. We will respond within a reasonable period.

3. What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted (or de-identified) after the grace period

  • Your profile: full name, phone number, email address, avatar.
  • Your password hash and all active sign-in sessions/tokens.
  • Your farm plots, boundaries and satellite (NDVI) observations.
  • Your harvest, livestock, sales and expense records.
  • Your produce listings and notification preferences.

Retained, in a limited and de-identified form

  • Financial and order records that we are required to keep for accounting/tax purposes may be retained for up to 84 months, with direct identifiers removed or replaced so they are no longer linked to you.
  • Security and audit logs may be kept for a short period to detect abuse, and to prove that a deletion was carried out.

Exact periods depend on the retention schedule in the AgriConnect Data Retention & Deletion policy and on applicable law. Deletion removes data from live systems; residual copies in encrypted backups expire on the backup retention cycle.

4. Questions

For anything about your data, contact privacy@agriconnect.bw. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
